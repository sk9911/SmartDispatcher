Forecast Regret

What does a weather forecast's error cost a transatlantic flight in fuel, time and CO₂?

1 · PLAN

Plan each flight on a forecast source's winds, 12 to 120 h before departure.

2 · FLY

Fly that plan through the truth: ERA5, the weather that actually happened.

3 · COMPARE

Regret = the extra fuel over the hindsight plan, the best route with the truth known.

120 crossings · JFK↔LHR · Jan–Feb 2020 · Boeing 787-9 at FL350, M0.85 · IFS HRES, GraphCast, Pangu-Weather, persistence

Forecast regret is the cost of not knowing the future. For every flight we plan a route on each forecast source at five lead times, fly every route through ERA5, and compare with the hindsight plan: the cheapest route among all candidates once the real weather is known. The difference is the regret, in fuel, time and CO2.

Up to 72 hours the three forecast sources are statistically tied, with overlapping confidence intervals, and regret is a few tens of kg at most. At 120 hours GraphCast and Pangu-Weather cost about half what IFS HRES costs: roughly 130 kg less fuel, 400 kg less CO2 and about a minute of flight time per crossing. The persistence baseline is far worse at every lead and sits off this chart. Medians are lower than means: a few bad flights pull the means up.
